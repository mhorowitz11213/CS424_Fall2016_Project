Headway Visualization Evaluation

Welcome

Thank you for participating in this study. This evaluation examines how people interpret public transportation headway data using different visualization designs.

You will complete four analytical tasks using two visualization conditions: a static visualization and an interactive visualization. Please answer each question based on the information displayed.

There are no time limits. However, we will record the time taken to complete each task. Please work independently and do not use external tools or calculators.

Your responses are anonymous. The questionnaire does not collect your name or email address. When you submit each answer, it is sent to a Google Sheet maintained by the research team, together with your anonymous participant ID, your answers, and the time taken.
